Questions people actually ask.

Will this get my Instagram account banned?

Nobody can promise you it won't, and you should be suspicious of any tool that does. Automated DMs are against Instagram's terms, and the risk is never zero.

What this extension does is give you the controls to send carefully and default them to cautious: a full dry run in Test Mode, gradual warm-up with Safe Start, a hard hourly cap, scheduled breaks, conservative pacing presets and automatic opt-outs. Start slow, watch the log, and raise the numbers only when nothing looks off.

Do I need a big following for this to work?

No. One customer had a single follower on the account, sent a hundred DMs, and had a lead the next day. Outreach doesn't depend on people finding you; it depends on you starting conversations with the right people.

Is the free plan a trial?

No. It's a permanent plan: one campaign and ten messages a day, no credit card, no expiry. Pro removes the caps for $29.99 a month and you can cancel from the Stripe billing portal whenever you like.

What happens to my data?

Campaigns, templates, target lists, counters, send memory and logs are stored locally in your browser. Our server keeps only a minimal licence record. Payments go through Stripe, so we never see your card. The optional AI features send the context you provide to Anthropic's Claude API; if you don't use them, nothing message-related leaves your machine. The privacy policy has every detail.

Does it keep running when I close my laptop?

No. It's a browser extension that works inside your own Instagram session, so it sends while Chrome is open. That's also why it behaves like you do rather than like a server farm. Progress is saved, so it picks up where it stopped.

Do I have to give you my Instagram password?

No. The extension works inside the Instagram tab you're already logged into, in your own browser. We never ask for, see or store your Instagram password.

Which browsers and computers does it work on?

Any desktop computer (Mac, Windows or Linux) running Google Chrome. Chromium-based browsers that install from the Chrome Web Store, like Brave and Arc, work too. It doesn't run on phones, because mobile browsers don't support extensions.

Is this affiliated with Instagram?

No. IG Outreach Pro is an independent tool built by Highpoint Scaling. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Instagram or Meta Platforms in any way.
