The real difference

Autolead is built around volume across accounts: you store many profiles, set a DM limit for each, and it switches to the next one when an account hits its daily maximum. Plans are capped by DMs per day until the $95/mo Enterprise tier.

IG Outreach Pro runs on your own account in your own Chrome, with no proxies. Pro is $29.99/mo with unlimited DMs, and adds follow-up sequences, reply scanning, AI drafting on the AI plan, and controls like Test Mode, pacing presets, an hourly cap and opt-out detection in 12 languages. We do not describe any tool, including ours, as ban proof.

Questions

Is Autolead cheaper than IG Outreach Pro?

At entry level, yes: Autolead Starter is $27/mo for 75 DMs a day. IG Outreach Pro's Pro plan is $29.99/mo with unlimited campaigns and DMs.

Does IG Outreach Pro use proxies?

No. It sends from your own browser on your own connection, so there is no proxy setup and no password shared.

Can anything make Instagram DMs ban proof?

No. Automated DMs can conflict with Instagram's terms, and no tool can honestly promise zero risk. Keeping volume modest and messages relevant lowers risk; it does not remove it.

How much does IG Outreach Pro cost?

Pro is $29.99/mo and includes unlimited campaigns and DMs plus multiple Instagram accounts. The AI plan, which adds AI message drafting from your tone and goal, is $49/mo.
