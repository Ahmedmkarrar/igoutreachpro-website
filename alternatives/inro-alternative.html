Pick Inro if you want comment-to-DM, an AI agent that answers messages and a CRM, all on Meta's official API. Pick IG Outreach Pro if you want to message new prospects who have not contacted you yet. They solve different problems, and many users run both.

The real difference

Inro is built for inbound: it turns comments, DMs and story mentions into conversations, answers with an AI agent and keeps contacts in a CRM, all through Meta's official API. Its DM campaigns go to contacts who are already in your Inro account.

IG Outreach Pro is built for outbound: it sends the first message to people who have not contacted you, such as followers of a relevant account or people posting a hashtag, and follows up until they reply. It runs as a Chrome extension on your own account rather than through the official API.

Because the jobs are different, many users run both: IG Outreach Pro to start new conversations, and Inro to handle replies and inbound engagement.

Questions

Is IG Outreach Pro a replacement for Inro?

Not really. Inro automates inbound conversations on Meta's official API. IG Outreach Pro sends outbound DMs to new prospects. Many users run both.

Does IG Outreach Pro integrate with Zapier or a CRM?

No. IG Outreach Pro offers CSV export of your send log. Inro offers Make, Zapier and webhook integrations.

Does IG Outreach Pro use Meta's official API?

No. It is a Chrome extension that runs on your own account in your own browser, with no cloud, no proxies and no password shared.

Can IG Outreach Pro guarantee my account will not be banned?

No. Automated DMs can conflict with Instagram's terms, and nobody can honestly promise zero risk. IG Outreach Pro gives you Test Mode, a Safe Start warm-up, pacing presets, an hourly cap, a break schedule and opt-out detection so you can keep volume and behavior conservative.
