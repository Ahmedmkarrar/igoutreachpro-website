Instagram follow-up messages: timing and examples

Send your first follow-up two to three days after an unanswered DM, a second one about a week after the first, and then stop. Each follow-up should be shorter than the last and give the person a new, easy reason to reply.

The short answer

  • First follow-up: two to three days after your first message.
  • Second follow-up: about five to seven days after the first follow-up.
  • Stop after two follow-ups. A third rarely helps and can annoy.
  • Keep follow-ups to one or two lines and do not repeat the first message.
  • Stop immediately if someone says no or asks you to stop.

Why follow-ups work

Most people who do not answer a first message are not saying no. They saw it at a bad moment, meant to come back and forgot, or never opened their Message Requests. A short follow-up gives them a second chance to answer.

There is no published Instagram data on follow-up rates, but cold email studies point the same way. A Backlinko and Pitchbox study of 12 million outreach emails found that one follow-up increased replies by 65.8%. Hunter's State of Cold Email report found campaigns with follow-ups averaged a 4.9% reply rate against 3% without, and that unsubscribes grew after the third follow-up. Email is a different channel, so treat this as direction, not a DM benchmark.

When to send each follow-up

Send at roughly the same time of day you would message a real contact, not at 3am in their time zone. If they viewed your story or liked a post in between, that is a natural moment to follow up.

  1. Day 0: first message.
  2. Day 2 or 3: first follow-up. Short, light, one new angle or question.
  3. Day 7 to 10: second follow-up. Even shorter. Often a simple close-the-loop line.
  4. After that: stop. If they come back later, pick it up from there.

Follow-up message examples

Swap in your own details. Each one is short on purpose.

  • Hey {firstname}, just bumping this in case it got buried in requests.
  • Quick one {firstname}: is weekday traffic something you are working on, or is it fine right now?
  • No worries if the timing is off. Would it help if I sent a two minute example instead?
  • Saw your post about the new menu, looks good. Still happy to share that idea if useful.
  • Should I close this out, or is it worth a quick chat next month?
  • Totally fine if this is not for you, just let me know and I will not message again.
  • Forgot to mention, I put together a short checklist for this. Want it?
  • Hey {firstname}, one last note from me. If it ever becomes a priority, my door is open.
  • Just checking: is there someone else on your team who handles this?
  • Did that question make sense, or should I explain it differently?

Tip. Write your follow-ups with spintax so they do not read identically across everyone in the sequence.

What not to do

  • Do not paste the first message again.
  • Do not guilt them: lines like 'I guess you are not interested in growing' end conversations.
  • Do not follow up daily.
  • Do not add a long pitch or price in a follow-up to someone who has not replied.
  • Do not keep going after a clear no, a block or a request to stop.

When they finally reply

Stop the sequence for that person straight away. Nothing looks worse than an automated 'just bumping this' arriving after someone has already answered.

Reply to what they actually said, ask one follow-up question about their situation, and only suggest a call once it is clear there is a real need. If they said not now, ask if you can check back in a set number of weeks, and note it.

Questions

How long should I wait before following up on Instagram?

Two to three days for the first follow-up is a reasonable default. Waiting less can feel pushy; waiting much longer means they have forgotten your first message.

How many times should I follow up?

Twice is a good rule. Email data from Hunter shows unsubscribes grow after the third follow-up, and on Instagram a decline or block costs you the contact entirely.

Is it okay to follow up if they saw my message but did not reply?

Yes, once or twice. A seen message only means they opened it. Keep the follow-up short and easy to answer, and accept silence after that.
